Frequently asked questions

Good to know before you start.

Quick answers about how Pipdojo works.

Do I need to connect my broker?

No. Pipdojo is designed for manual trade logging. You enter your trades yourself and review the decisions behind them.

Can other traders see my trades?

Your journal is intended to be private to your account. Production privacy and database access controls must be fully configured and tested before public launch.

Does Pipdojo place trades or provide signals?

No. Pipdojo is a journaling and performance-review tool. It does not execute trades, provide signals or guarantee trading results.

Can I use Pipdojo on my phone?

The website is designed to adapt to desktop, tablet and mobile screens. You can open it in a modern browser.

Does Pipdojo guarantee better trading results?

No. A journal can help you understand your own process, but it cannot guarantee profits or remove the risks of trading.
