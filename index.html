What you can log and review

Every trade in R

Record the outcome and the R you reached, so trades of different sizes can be compared fairly.

Built around your own system

Rename the groups (session, pair, setup, level, time frame), add your own options and switch off what you do not use.

Your emotions and your mistakes

Note how you felt, and whether you followed your plan, moved your stop loss or entered early.

A review after each trade

Write what you did well and the lesson for next time, while the trade is still fresh.

Your own lists

Rename or add setups, sessions and other options, so the journal matches your strategy.

The same journal on every device

Log in on your phone or your laptop and see the same trades. Download a backup file whenever you want.

Questions

Do I need to connect my broker?

No. You add each trade yourself, which also makes you think about it.

Can other traders see my trades?

No. Each account only sees its own trades.

Does it work on my phone?

Yes. Open Pipdojo in your phone’s browser and log in.

Does Pipdojo place trades or give signals?

No. It is a journal. It does not trade for you and it does not give financial advice.

What does it cost?

You can start for free. Pro plans are coming later.
